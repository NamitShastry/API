AeroIndex / FareOS Mathematical & Governance Methodology

Official regulatory specification for high-frequency domestic airfare tracking in India, replacing manual CPI air travel sampling with transparent, un-biased index compilation.

1. The Elementary Jevons Index Formula

At the elementary cell level (Route $r \times$ Lead Bucket $b \times$ Day-of-Week $w$), the unweighted Jevons price relative avoids the upward substitution bias of Dutot and Carli formulations:

I_Jevons(t, 0) = [ ∏_{i=1}^n ( p_{i,t} / p_{i,0} ) ]^(1/n) = [ exp( (1/n) * ∑_{i=1}^n ln(p_{i,t}) ) / exp( (1/n) * ∑_{i=1}^n ln(p_{i,0}) ) ] × 100

2. Basket Weight Aggregation & Coverage Guard

Elementary cells are aggregated to the National Composite Index using DGCA passenger traffic route shares ($W_r$) and lead-time distribution weights ($w_b$). A strict minimum 80% coverage guard is required before official publication.

3. Dual Real-Time Architecture

The system strictly separates the continuous observation layer from scheduled collection. Observations continuously update the FLASH Redis engine for live distribution, while the 4 daily cycles (00:30, 06:30, 12:30, 18:30 IST) establish the frozen settlement index at 23:30 IST.
